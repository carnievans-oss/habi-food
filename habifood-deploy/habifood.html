⚠ Safety & PPE — Read Before Collecting
🧤 Wear gardening gloves when handling branches
👟 Closed shoes — no thongs near trees or roadsides
🕶 Eye protection when cutting at head height
🚗 Pull fully off the road before stopping. Do not collect from live traffic lanes.
⚡ Do not collect from trees under powerlines without a spotter
🐝 Check branches for insects, bird nests and spider webs before bagging
Step 1 — Animals in care right now
📋 Collection Summary
Visual reference for anyone collecting browse — carer or volunteer. How to identify each food tree, signs of stress or disease, and when to leave the branch on the tree.
⚠ Universal signs — never collect if you see these
🚫 Oily or waxy film on leaves — chemical treatment. Walk away, note location, report to carer.
🚫 Unusually strong or chemical smell — herbicide or pesticide application. Do not collect.
🚫 Orange or yellow powdery pustules on leaf undersides — Myrtle rust. Affects all Myrtaceae (eucalypts, melaleucas, paperbarks). Do not collect. Report.
🚫 Distorted, thickened or abnormally coloured new growth — possible fungal infection or severe pest load.
🚫 Foam, unusual deposits or residue on leaves or bark — unknown origin. Avoid.
🚫 Freshly cut stumps or obvious recent pruning nearby — possible recent council treatment. Collect from unaffected branches only.
🟡 Brown or crispy leaf tips only — drought stress on tips. Collect from unaffected branches further from tips. Animal should be offered moist alternatives alongside.
